# Genre playbook

**rxz** · what each genre promises, and the shot that keeps it.

Genre is not decoration. It tells you the *promise* (what the first minute feels like), the *archetype* (where the
eye goes) and the *palette* (what the feed will read as this kind of game). Use the row, then write a specific
brief — the trap is making the genre's stock image rather than this game's.

| Genre | Promise to show | Archetype | Palette | Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Obby | "I can make that jump" | Action Freeze | Bright sky + hazard red | `DON'T FALL` |
| Tycoon | "This empire is mine" | Hero Shot | Gold + one dark ground | `SOLD OUT` |
| Simulator / pets | "Look what I hatched" | Object as Hero | Candy pastel + one rare hue | `NEW PET` |
| Horror | "Something is behind me" | Threat Behind | Desaturated blue + one warm point | none, or `RUN` |
| Battlegrounds | "I win this fight" | Versus or Hero | High-contrast team colours | `1V1` |
| Roleplay / life | "I live here now" | Hero Shot, wide | Warm interior light | none |
| Racing | "I'm already gone" | Action Freeze | Sunset orange + asphalt | `200 MPH` |
| Tower defence | "They will not pass" | Object as Hero / Hero | Night + tracer colour | `WAVE 50` |
| Anime / gacha | "I pulled the one" | Hero Shot, low angle | One saturated hue + white | `SS TIER` |
| "Steal a ___" | "I'm taking yours" | Versus / Action | Two clashing hues | `STOLEN` |
| Hangout / UGC | "This is my place" | Hero Shot, group-safe | Soft daylight + brand colour | none |

## Obby

- **Hero:** the player mid-jump, arms out, feet off the ground.
- **Prop:** one lethal-looking obstacle, close enough to read as a threat.
- **Camera:** low, wide, tilted a few degrees; the gap below is the story.
- **Light:** warm key, bright sky behind, subject rimmed.
- **Fail:** a static platform panorama. It is beautiful and promises nothing.
- **Shot idea:** "the last jump" — one gap, one lava pool, the finish flag just out of reach.

## Tycoon

- **Hero:** the player, proud, holding the first cash stack.
- **Prop:** the upgrade they just bought — a shop sign, a conveyor, a safe.
- **Camera:** low angle, subject in a third, the build rising behind.
- **Light:** golden hour, hard shadow, one cool fill.
- **Fail:** a top-down screenshot of the whole base. Reads as a map, not a fantasy.
- **Shot idea:** "first sale" — one small shop, a queue of customers, the owner grinning.

## Simulator / pets

- **Hero:** the rarest pet, giant, at 3/4, with a hard contact shadow.
- **Prop:** none needed; scale is the prop.
- **Camera:** top-down 3/4, subject filling half the frame.
- **Light:** candy-bright, rim glow in the pet's own colour.
- **Fail:** five pets in a row. At tile size they become a texture.
- **Shot idea:** "the hatch" — one Mythic pet bursting out of an egg, shell fragments mid-air.

## Horror

- **Hero:** the player, seen from behind or 3/4, fear readable in the shoulders.
- **Prop:** a dying light — flashlight, match, phone.
- **Camera:** wide, low, foreground object framing one edge.
- **Light:** cold ambient, one warm pool on the hero, the threat lit separately.
- **Fail:** a dark image with no readable threat. Darkness is not tension.
- **Shot idea:** "the hallway" — hero in the foreground, one eye-lit shape at the far end.

## Battlegrounds / PvP

- **Hero:** your fighter, mid-strike, face set.
- **Prop:** the weapon, clear of the body silhouette.
- **Camera:** low, 35 mm, subject slightly off-centre.
- **Light:** hard key, coloured rim per team.
- **Fail:** two characters shaking hands or posing. Nobody clicks a handshake.
- **Shot idea:** "the last hit" — one fighter landing, the other already off-balance.

## Roleplay / life

- **Hero:** the player in an outfit that reads as a role (chef, officer, idol).
- **Prop:** one object that names the fantasy — a tray, a guitar, a badge.
- **Camera:** eye level, 50 mm, warm and close.
- **Light:** soft interior, practical lamps, one window.
- **Fail:** a group photo. It reads as a Discord banner.
- **Shot idea:** "my first shift" — one character mid-task in a warm room that looks lived in.

## Racing

- **Hero:** the car, 3/4 front, one wheel off the ground, or the player leaning into a turn.
- **Prop:** the road, the barrier, the finish line.
- **Camera:** low and close, wide lens, slight tilt.
- **Light:** sunset backlight, dust catching the rim.
- **Fail:** a car parked. Speed is the entire product.
- **Shot idea:** "the shortcut" — mid-air over a gap, dust behind, finish banner ahead.

## Tower defence

- **Hero:** the strongest tower or the commander, at a slight low angle.
- **Prop:** one wave of enemies, small and numerous, at the frame's edge.
- **Camera:** 3/4 wide, tower in a third, lane leading in.
- **Light:** night blue, tracer orange, one green rim.
- **Fail:** a clean diagram of the map. Strategy screenshots are not thumbnails.
- **Shot idea:** "wave 50" — one tower firing, a wall of enemies, the base behind it.

## Anime / gacha

- **Hero:** the character, low angle, hair and coat moving.
- **Prop:** the aura or weapon, not both.
- **Camera:** low, 35 mm, subject in a third, sky behind.
- **Light:** one saturated key, white rim, deep shadow.
- **Fail:** a neutral standing pose with a gradient background. That is the slop template.
- **Shot idea:** "the pull" — the character landing, aura breaking, a single rarity glyph.

## "Steal a ___"

- **Hero:** the thief, mid-grab, gleeful.
- **Prop:** the thing being stolen, large, recognisable, in the other hand.
- **Camera:** two-shot diagonal, hands in the middle.
- **Light:** two clashing keys, one per side.
- **Fail:** the exact template every clone uses — same pose, same arrow, same font.
- **Shot idea:** "the getaway" — the thief already running, the owner's hands closing on air.

## Hangout / UGC

- **Hero:** the player, relaxed, in the space's signature spot.
- **Prop:** one item that only exists in this game.
- **Camera:** wide, eye level, room visible behind.
- **Light:** daylight or neon signage, consistent direction.
- **Fail:** an empty room. Hangouts sell the crowd, not the architecture.
- **Shot idea:** "the rooftop" — one character at the rail, city behind, a second arriving.

## The ten-slot set

Rotate archetypes so the gallery does not read as one asset recoloured:

| Slot | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hero Shot — the promise, fastest read |
| 2 | Gameplay proof — the loop actually happening |
| 3 | Object as Hero — the reward |
| 4 | Action Freeze — movement and feel |
| 5 | Threat / Versus — the stakes |
| 6 | Before / After — progression |
| 7 | A "what just happened" moment — social proof |
| 8 | Update art — new content, dated |
| 9 | Community — a real player's moment (with permission) |
| 10 | Wildcard — the one that breaks the pattern on purpose |

Slot 1 is the ad. Everything else supports it or tests it.
